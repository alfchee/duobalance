# Where to start: a 5-minute guide to getting your finances in order

Tracking your money doesn't have to be complicated. This guide walks you through the first steps — in five minutes and without budgets for now.

## Why track first

A budget set without real data rarely sticks. Logging one or two weeks of spending shows you where the money goes before you decide how much to allocate.

- **Week 1–2:** Just record. Don't limit, just observe.
- **Week 3:** Look at totals by category and decide what to adjust.

## A seven-day plan

A gentle seven-day start — no streaks, no penalties for missed days.

### Day 1: Log a recent purchase

Open DuoBalance and log a recent purchase — coffee, groceries, transit. It takes seconds.

### Day 2–3: Add two more

Add one or two expenses per day. You'll see your balances update and categories take shape.

### Day 4–5: Invite your partner

From Settings → Household members you can invite your partner. Do a monthly review together, not a per-purchase check.

### Day 6–7: Observe, don't judge

At the end of the week, open Reports and look at your totals. Just observe — this isn't the moment to fix everything.

## What to ignore for now

For these two weeks you can ignore the Budget tab. Creating limits without data is guessing. Come back when you have real numbers.
