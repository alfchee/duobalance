# Reading your first month of data

Once you have two to four weeks of entries, the numbers start to tell a story. This lesson shows how to read it without overthinking.

## The monthly total

In Reports, select the current month. The headline number is your net — income minus expenses — converted to your household's base currency.

## By category

The bar chart groups spending by category. Look for the two or three biggest bars — that's where a small change has the biggest effect.

## What not to do yet

Don't set a dozen budgets at once. Pick the one or two categories that surprised you and set a soft limit there first.
