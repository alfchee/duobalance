# Who spent it vs. who recorded it

When logging a transaction in duobalance, we distinguish between **Who spent it (spent_by)** and **Who recorded it (entered_by)**.

## Why this distinction matters

It is common for one partner to enter receipts for both people at the end of the day or week.

- **Spent by (spent_by):** Indicates whose purchase or responsibility it was. Used for filtering personal reports and individual budgets.
- **Recorded by (entered_by):** Automatically assigned to the active user session that created the transaction entry.

## Correcting attribution

You can edit any existing transaction at any time to update the "Spent by" field if it was assigned incorrectly.
