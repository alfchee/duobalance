# Why does an exchange rate show a warning?

When logging transactions in currencies different from your base currency, duobalance applies official exchange rates.

## What the warning means

If exchange rates haven't been refreshed in over 24 hours (for instance, over weekends or banking holidays), an informational badge appears.

- **Impact:** You can continue logging transactions normally.
- **Resolution:** Rates refresh automatically on the next daily background job, or you can manually override the exchange rate on any transaction.
