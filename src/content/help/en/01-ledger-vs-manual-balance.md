# Ledger vs. manual balance mode

In duobalance each account operates in one of two balance modes: **Ledger** or **Manual**.

## Ledger Mode

In Ledger mode, the account balance is computed automatically from the opening balance plus all transactions linked to that account.

- **When to use:** For main accounts where you record every expense, income, or transfer.
- **Why it might differ:** If a transaction is missing or the initial balance was set incorrectly.

## Manual Balance Mode

In Manual mode, you directly set the current balance by updating it periodically.

- **When to use:** For credit cards, investment accounts, or secondary accounts where you don't want to track every small purchase.
- **Transactions in manual mode:** You can still log transactions so they count toward budgets and reports, but they won't automatically alter the manually set balance.

## How to switch modes

1. Go to **Balances** or **Account Settings**.
2. Tap the account you wish to modify.
3. Select your preferred balance mode and save changes.
