# Using the mobile menu and quick transaction entry

On a phone, tap the round menu button in the lower-left corner to open the navigation drawer. It includes Balances, Transactions, Budget, Reports, Bills, Settings, Help, and sign out. Use **New transaction** at the bottom of the drawer to open quick entry.

## Description suggestions

When entering a transaction description, suggestions appear after you type at least two characters. Choose a matching previous description to reuse it, or keep typing to enter a new one. Up to six suggestions are shown above the field so the on-screen keyboard does not cover them.

## Save a transaction

Enter the amount, description, account, category, and who spent it, then save. You can still add notes or other details when needed.
