# Household vs. personal budgets

duobalance allows you to organize budgets into two scopes: **Household** and **My Budgets**.

## Household Budgets

Track shared living costs (such as rent, groceries, and utilities). Both partners contribute toward the limit and view shared progress.

## Personal Budgets

Private to each member (e.g., hobbies or personal care). They let you track individual spending without affecting global household limits.

## Switching tabs

Use the toggle at the top of the Budget screen to switch between **Household** and **Mine** views.
