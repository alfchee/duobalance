# How to switch households and manage members

You can belong to more than one household. Each household has its own accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, members, and settings.

## Switching or creating a household

1. Tap the current household name at the top of the app.
2. Select another household to open its data.
3. In the same window, choose **Create household** to start another workspace or **Join with invitation** to enter one with an invitation code.

The app clears the previous household's temporary screens and selections when you switch, so data is never mixed between households. You can create up to five households.

## Inviting and managing members

Owners can open **Settings** and use **Household Members** to send, resend, or revoke invitations. An owner can also transfer ownership to a partner. Choose whether to remain an owner or become a partner after the transfer.

Only an owner can remove a partner. Before confirming removal, decide what happens to each shared account owned by that partner: transfer it to yourself or make it joint. The removed member loses access to the household; their historical entries stay available to the household.

## Leaving or deleting a household

Open **Settings** and scroll to the danger zone to leave a household. An owner must transfer ownership before leaving while other members remain. The final member can leave.

Owners can also delete a household. Type its exact name to confirm. Deletion is recoverable for 30 days, but export the household first if you need a personal copy.
