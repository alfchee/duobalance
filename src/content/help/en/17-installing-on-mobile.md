# How to install duobalance on iPhone or Android

duobalance runs as a Progressive Web App (PWA), letting you install it to your phone's Home Screen without an app store download.

## On iPhone (iOS)

1. Open Safari and navigate to duobalance.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

## On Android

1. Open Chrome or another supported browser.
2. Tap the three-dot menu icon in the upper-right corner.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
