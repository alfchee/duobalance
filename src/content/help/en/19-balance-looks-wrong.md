# What to do if a balance looks wrong

If you notice a discrepancy between duobalance and your actual bank statement, follow these troubleshooting steps.

## Step-by-step diagnostic

1. **Check the balance mode:** Confirm whether the account is in **Ledger** or **Manual** mode.
2. **Review the opening balance:** Go to **Balances**, edit the account, and verify that the initial balance matched your real balance on the starting date.
3. **Look for missing transactions:** Ensure no recent transactions were skipped.
4. **Check transfer entries:** Verify whether a transfer was accidentally logged as a regular expense.
