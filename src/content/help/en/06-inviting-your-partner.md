# How to invite your partner to duobalance

Managing household finances works best when both partners have access to the same household workspace.

## Steps to send an invitation

1. Navigate to **Settings** and open **Household Members**.
2. Enter your partner's email address.
3. Click **Send Invitation**.

## How your partner joins

- Your partner will receive an email invitation link.
- Clicking the link and signing in or registering will automatically link them to your household, granting access to shared accounts and budgets immediately.
