# Why aren't bill reminders arriving?

If you set up a bill reminder but haven't received a push or email notification, check the following reasons.

## iOS push notification requirements

On iOS, web push notifications require the app to be installed as a PWA on your Home Screen and your phone to be running iOS 16.4 or later.

- **Solution:** Follow the guide at [/install](/install) to add duobalance to your Home Screen.

## Guaranteed email reminders

To ensure you never miss a due date, duobalance also sends email notifications. Check your spam folder and mark duobalance emails as trusted.
