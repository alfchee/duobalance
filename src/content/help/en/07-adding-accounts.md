# Adding accounts: types, currencies, and privacy

You can track bank accounts, credit cards, cash, and investments in duobalance.

## Steps to create an account

1. Go to **Balances** and click **+ New Account**.
2. Fill in the account details:
   - **Name:** A descriptive title (e.g., "Checking Account" or "Cash Wallet").
   - **Type:** Checking, savings, credit card, cash, or investment.
   - **Currency:** Choose the native currency of the account.
   - **Visibility:** Choose **Shared** (visible to your partner) or **Private**.
   - **Balance mode:** Select **Ledger** or **Manual**.
3. Enter the **Opening Balance** and save changes.
