# Exporting your financial data

Your data belongs to you. You can export or back up your complete transaction and account history at any time.

## Steps to export

1. Go to **Settings** and scroll down to **Data & Privacy**.
2. Select **Export Data**.
3. Choose your desired format:
   - **CSV:** Best for opening in spreadsheets like Excel or Google Sheets.
   - **JSON:** Complete structured backup format.
4. Click **Download File**.
