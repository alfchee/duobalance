# Who spent it vs. who recorded it?

When logging a transaction in duobalance, we distinguish between **Who spent it (spent_by)** and **Who recorded it (entered_by)**.

## Why distinguish between them?

It is very common for one partner to enter receipts or invoices for both people at the end of the day or week.

- **Spent by (spent_by):** Indicates whose purchase or responsibility it was. Used for personal reports and individual budgets.
- **Recorded by (entered_by):** Automatically assigned to the active user who created the transaction entry.

## Correcting attribution

You can edit an existing transaction at any time to change the "Spent by" selection if it was misassigned.
