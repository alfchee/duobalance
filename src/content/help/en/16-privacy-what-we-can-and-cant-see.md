# Privacy: what we can and cannot see

At duobalance, financial privacy and data security are foundational principles.

## What your partner sees

- Your partner can only see accounts set as **Shared** and transactions associated with those accounts.
- Your **Private** accounts and their detailed transaction histories remain invisible to your partner.

## What the platform sees

- duobalance uses encryption and database-level Row Level Security (RLS) policies so only authorized members of your household can access your data.
- We do not sell or share your personal financial data with advertisers or third parties.
