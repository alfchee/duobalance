# Private accounts and balance visibility

In duobalance you can choose which accounts are shared with your household and which remain private.

## Views: All, My Accounts, and Shared

- **Shared:** Both household members can view the balance, history, and individual transactions of this account.
- **Private (Mine):** Only the account owner can view detailed balances and individual line items.

## Why do my partner and I see different totals?

If you hold a private account, your personal total ("All" or "Mine" view) includes your private funds, while your partner's view does not. This is by design to respect individual financial privacy within the household.

## Private transactions in Budgets

Transactions made from a private account can still be assigned to household budgets if you choose to categorize them as shared expenses.
