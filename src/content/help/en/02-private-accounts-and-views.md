# Private accounts and balance visibility

In duobalance you can choose which accounts are shared with the household and which remain private.

## Views: All, Mine, and Shared

- **Shared:** Both members of the household can view the balance, history, and transactions.
- **Private (Mine):** Only the account owner can view detailed balances and individual movements.

## Why do my partner and I see different totals?

If you have a private account, your personal total ("All" or "Mine" view) will include your private funds, while your partner's view will not. This is by design to respect individual financial privacy within the household.

## Private transactions in Budgets

Transactions made from a private account can still be assigned to household budgets if you choose to categorize them as a shared expense.
