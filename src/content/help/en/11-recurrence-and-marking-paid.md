# Recurring bills and marking as paid

The Bills section helps you keep track of fixed, recurring obligations such as rent, subscriptions, or utility payments.

## Setting up recurrence

When creating a bill, select the repeat cycle (monthly, biweekly, annually, etc.). duobalance will automatically generate the upcoming bill instance for each period.

## Marking as paid

When you pay a bill:

1. Tap the pending bill.
2. Select **Mark as Paid**.
3. Choose whether to automatically record a corresponding expense transaction from one of your accounts.
