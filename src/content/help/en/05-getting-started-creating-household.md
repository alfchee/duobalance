# Getting started: creating your household

A household in duobalance is the shared workspace where you and your partner manage finances together.

## Initial setup

1. Create your account with your email address.
2. Upon first signing in, name your household (e.g., "Taylor & Alex Household").
3. Select your household's **Base Currency**. All aggregate reports will consolidate totals into this currency.

## Recommended next steps

- Invite your partner under **Household Settings**.
- Add your main bank or cash accounts under **Balances**.
- Set up core category limits under **Budget**.
