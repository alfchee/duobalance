# How to read charts and reports

The Reports section turns your transactions into clear financial insights for the household.

## Category Breakdown Chart

Shows where most of your expenses are concentrated for the selected date range.

## Monthly Trends & Income vs. Expense

Compares total cash inflow and outflow month by month.

- **Green bars:** Total income.
- **Red bars:** Total expenses.

## Currency conversion in reports

All amounts are converted to your household's base currency using exchange rates on the transaction date.
