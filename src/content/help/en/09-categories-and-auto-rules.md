# Categories and auto-categorization rules

Categories organize your transactions so budgets and reports reflect your actual spending habits.

## Default and custom categories

duobalance includes standard categories like Food & Dining, Utilities, Transportation, and Housing. You can add or edit custom categories under **Settings > Categories**.

## Auto-categorization rules

Auto-rules allow you to map merchant descriptions to categories automatically:

- **Creating rules:** When logging a recurring vendor (e.g., "Grocery Store X"), you can save an auto-rule so future entries matching that keyword automatically populate the right category.
