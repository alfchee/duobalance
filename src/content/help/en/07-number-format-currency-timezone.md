# Currency, number format, and timezone settings

You can customize how duobalance presents numbers and dates in the **Settings** section.

## Household Base Currency

The primary currency used to calculate household totals and reports.

## Number Format

Choose between period separators (`1,234.56`) or comma separators (`1.234,56`) according to your regional preference.

## Timezone

Ensures month cutoffs and bill due dates are calculated according to your local time.
