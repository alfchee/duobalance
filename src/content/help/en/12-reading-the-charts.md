# How to read charts and reports

The Reports section turns your raw transactions into clear financial insights for your household.

## Category Breakdown Chart

Shows which categories account for the largest portion of your spending during the selected date range.

## Monthly Trend and Income vs. Expense

Compares total money flowing in versus money going out month over month.

- **Green bars:** Total income.
- **Red bars:** Total expenses.

## Currency conversion in reports

All foreign currency amounts are converted to your household's base currency using exchange rates recorded on the transaction dates.
